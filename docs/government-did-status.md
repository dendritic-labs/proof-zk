# Government Digital Identity Initiatives - DID Status

## 🏛️ Governments Issuing Digital Identity Credentials

### **🇪🇺 European Union - eIDAS 2.0**
- **Status**: Regulation passed, implementation by 2026
- **DID Support**: W3C DID standards mandated
- **Scope**: Digital wallets for all EU citizens
- **Credentials**: ID cards, passports, driver's licenses, health certificates
- **Airlines Impact**: Will require airlines to accept EU digital wallets

### **🇪🇪 Estonia - e-Residency**
- **Status**: Live since 2014, expanding
- **DID Support**: Custom digital identity system (moving toward W3C standards)
- **Scope**: Digital citizenship with government services
- **Credentials**: Digital ID, business registration, tax certificates
- **Travel**: Digital nomad visas with cryptographic verification

### **🇸🇬 Singapore - SingPass**
- **Status**: Live, 4+ million users
- **DID Support**: Moving toward W3C standards
- **Scope**: National digital identity platform
- **Credentials**: NRIC, work permits, vaccination certificates
- **Travel**: Digital health certificates for travel

### **🇺🇸 United States - Multiple Initiatives**

**DHS/TSA - Digital Identity Programs**
- **Status**: Pilot programs, expanding 2024-2025
- **DID Support**: Exploring W3C standards
- **Scope**: TSA PreCheck, Global Entry, Real ID
- **Travel**: Mobile driver's licenses (mDL) accepted at airports

**State-Level mDL Programs**
- **Status**: 15+ states launched, 30+ by 2025
- **Standards**: ISO 18013-5 (mobile driver's license)
- **Airlines**: Delta, United piloting mDL acceptance

### **🇨🇦 Canada - Digital Identity Initiative**
- **Status**: Federal framework development
- **DID Support**: Evaluating W3C standards
- **Scope**: Digital credentials across provinces
- **Travel**: Digital passport pilot with Passport Canada

### **🇦🇺 Australia - Digital Identity Program**
- **Status**: myGovID expanding, DTA digital identity framework
- **DID Support**: Considering W3C standards
- **Scope**: Government services, banking, travel
- **Travel**: Digital travel credentials pilot

### **🇬🇧 United Kingdom - GOV.UK Verify Evolution**
- **Status**: Transitioning to new digital identity framework
- **DID Support**: Exploring W3C standards
- **Scope**: Post-Brexit digital identity strategy
- **Travel**: Digital immigration status integration

### **🇫🇷 France - FranceConnect+**
- **Status**: Expanding beyond government services
- **DID Support**: EU eIDAS 2.0 compliance
- **Scope**: Citizens' digital wallet
- **Travel**: Integration with EU travel credentials

### **🇩🇪 Germany - ID Wallet**
- **Status**: Federal digital wallet development
- **DID Support**: EU eIDAS 2.0 implementation
- **Scope**: Digital ID card, driver's license, health insurance
- **Travel**: Schengen area digital credentials

## 🛂 International Standards

### **ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization)**
- **Doc 9303**: Machine-readable travel documents
- **PKI Integration**: Digital signatures for e-passports
- **DID Exploration**: Working group on decentralized identity

### **ISO Standards**
- **ISO 18013-5**: Mobile driver's license (mDL)
- **ISO 23220**: Building blocks for identity management
- **ISO 27001**: Information security for government credentials

### **W3C Standards**
- **Verifiable Credentials**: Government credential format
- **DID Core**: Decentralized identifier specification
- **VP (Verifiable Presentations)**: Travel authorization format
